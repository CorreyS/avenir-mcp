This tour follows a real conversation on the demo plan, an invented household with a
checking and a savings account. For each step you see what you say, which tool the agent
calls with which arguments, what avenir-mcp answers, and what the agent tells you. Your own
answers will have the same shape, with your own figures.

<Aside type="tip">
You never name tools yourself: say what you want, the agent picks the tool. Naming a
tool (*“use get_monthly_summary”*) works too, and helps when an answer surprises you.
</Aside>

## 1. Look around

> **You:** What accounts do I have, and how much is on them?

The agent calls `list_accounts` with `{"plan_id": "last-used"}`:

<Code code={accounts} lang="json" title="list_accounts" />

> **Claude:** In the budget, Checking at 2,534.48, all cleared, and Savings at 600.00.
> Tracked outside it: the joint savings at 16,342.36, and the car loan with 19,040.81
> still owed. The student loan, paid off, and the former bank are closed.

Balances are in your plan's currency. `cleared_balance` is what the bank has already
shown; `uncleared_balance` is what it has not.

## 2. See how the month is going

> **You:** How is September going?

The agent calls `get_monthly_summary` with `{"plan_id": "last-used", "month":
"2026-09-01"}`:

<Code code={summary} lang="json" title="get_monthly_summary" />

> **Claude:** September is spending as planned except Restaurants, overspent by 22.50.
> Nothing is left to assign: Ready to Assign is at 0.00.

Only overspent categories are listed. For the detail, ask for it:

> **You:** Show me every category for September.

<Code code={balances} lang="json" title="get_category_balances (first entries)" />

`activity` is what was spent (negative) or received; `balance` is what is still
available. Categories with nothing budgeted, spent or available are left out unless the
agent passes `include_empty`.

## 3. Tidy up

> **You:** What still needs a category?

The agent calls `suggest_categories` with `{"plan_id": "last-used"}`:

<Code code={pending} lang="json" title="suggest_categories (first page, 3 items)" />

> **Claude:** Six transactions are waiting. Five have a clear suggestion from your
> history: Market Fresh twice, Streamflix, Rail Co… The bakery is new to me: groceries?

Where your history is clear, a `suggestion` is ready with its `confidence`. For a
merchant never seen before, the agent proposes a category from the `categories` list and
asks you.

## 4. Change something — safely

> **You:** Yes, the bakery is groceries, and the train is transport.

The agent calls `apply_categories` with the two assignments. **Nothing changes yet:**

<Code code={preview} lang="json" title="apply_categories — preview" />

<Steps>

1. avenir-mcp computed exactly what would change: two transactions, from no category to
   Groceries and Transport.
2. Your client shows you the question — a confirmation box if it supports MCP
   elicitation, or the agent relays the preview and waits.
3. You say yes. The agent calls again with your answer (or the `confirmation` code).
4. avenir-mcp applies both changes in one YNAB request, records them in its journal, and
   returns an `operation_id`.

</Steps>

> **You:** Actually, undo that.

The agent calls `undo_operation`, which previews putting both transactions back where
they were, and applies it once you agree. See [Undo a change](/avenir-mcp/guides/undo/).

## 5. Use the ready-made workflows

Instead of steering each step, start a prompt: it tells the agent the whole method.

| Prompt | What it walks you through |
|---|---|
| `classify_pending` | suggestions, your choices, one confirmed batch, the undo id |
| `monthly_review` | totals, what stands out, proposed fixes |
| `reconcile` | the gap with your bank explained, then a confirmed reconciliation |
| `plan_next_month` | a forecast and its assumptions, then next month's amounts |

In Claude Code they appear among the slash commands of the `avenir-mcp` server.
