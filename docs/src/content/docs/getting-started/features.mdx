avenir-mcp gives an agent 37 tools, 4 resources and 4 prompts over your YNAB plans. This
page lists them by what you want to do. Each row gives a sentence you can say to the
agent; the agent chooses the tool.

<Aside type="note">
**R** = read-only, always available. **W** = write, available with
`AVENIR_MCP_WRITE=1`, previewed and confirmed. **↺** = can be undone with
`undo_operation`.
</Aside>

<Aside type="tip" title="Plan or budget?">
YNAB's [API changelog](https://api.ynab.com/#changelog) records that v1.79.0 (2026-03-05)
renamed `/budgets` to `/plans`, and so do avenir-mcp's tools (`list_plans`,
`plan_id`). Say whichever you prefer: the agent understands both and answers with your
word. "My budget for Restaurants" still means the amount assigned to that category.
</Aside>

## Classify transactions

| You say | Tool | |
|---|---|---|
| *“Fetch my bank's latest transactions.”* | [`import_transactions`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/import-transactions/) — what Import does in YNAB, applied at once | W |
| *“What still needs a category?”* | [`suggest_categories`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/suggest-categories/) — pending transactions, with a suggestion when your history is clear | R |
| *“Yes, apply that.”* | [`apply_categories`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/apply-categories/) — one confirmed batch | W ↺ |
| *“Flag the ones I should check in YNAB.”* | [`flag_transactions`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/flag-transactions/) — a coloured flag, to look at in YNAB | W ↺ |
| *“Which payees are just bank labels?”* | [`list_payees`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/list-payees/) — every payee with the merchant its label normalises to, and how often it is used | R |
| *“Call that one Market Fresh.”* | [`rename_payee`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/rename-payee/) — a label becomes the merchant's name, for its whole history | W ↺ |
| *“Split this receipt: 81.15 groceries, 5.25 household.”* | [`split_transaction`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/split-transaction/) — one transaction across several categories; undone in YNAB, not here | W |
| *“Mark them as reviewed.”* | [`approve_transactions`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/approve-transactions/) — clears YNAB's *unapproved* flag | W |

Suggestions recognise a merchant across its bank labels (`CB MARKET FRESH FACT 050926
525130******1` and `… 190926 …` are the same shop), learn money in and money out apart,
and never point to a hidden category. Guide:
[Classify pending transactions](/avenir-mcp/guides/classify/). How it works:
[Suggestions](/avenir-mcp/concepts/suggestions/).

## Check against the bank

| You say | Tool | |
|---|---|---|
| *“My bank shows 3,440.80. Does YNAB agree?”* | [`reconcile_account`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/reconcile-account/) — explains a gap without writing; reconciles once it matches | W ↺ |
| *“Add the pharmacy payment the import missed.”* | [`create_transactions`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/create-transactions/) | W ↺ |

A gap is explained by pending transactions, the one whose amount equals the difference,
and likely duplicates (same amount and merchant, at most 3 days apart). Guides:
[Reconcile an account](/avenir-mcp/guides/reconcile/),
[Add missing transactions](/avenir-mcp/guides/missing-transactions/).

## Plan

| You say | Tool | |
|---|---|---|
| *“Will I go below zero before December?”* | [`forecast_balance`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/forecast-balance/) — month by month, with its assumptions | R |
| *“Am I paying off my debts?”* | [`get_net_worth_trend`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/get-net-worth-trend/) — assets, debts and net worth at each month end, tracking accounts included | R |
| *“If I lost my income, how long could I last?”* | [`get_runway`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/get-runway/) — months the money in the budget would cover the usual spending, essentials apart | R |
| *“Which big bills are coming this year, and am I ready?”* | [`get_upcoming_expenses`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/get-upcoming-expenses/) — yearly, half-yearly and quarterly bills, scheduled or found in the history, and whether each category will hold enough in time | R |
| *“What comes out before payday, and how low will checking get?”* | [`get_paycheck_plan`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/get-paycheck-plan/) — the next paycheck from the history, the bills before it and the lowest balance, day by day | R |
| *“When will my debts be paid off if I put 500 a month on them?”* | [`get_debt_payoff_plan`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/get-debt-payoff-plan/) — avalanche or snowball, the month each debt is paid off and the interest each order costs | R |
| *“Which categories will I overspend this month?”* | [`get_category_pace`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/get-category-pace/) — each category's spending projected to the end of the month from its own rhythm, those likely to run out first | R |
| *“When will Holidays reach 2,000?”* | [`get_goal_projection`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/get-goal-projection/) — the month a category reaches an amount at its usual pace, and what a month needs to reach it by a date | R |
| *“Put 150 in Restaurants for September.”* | [`set_category_budget`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/set-category-budget/) | W ↺ |
| *“Move 30 from Tennis to Restaurants.”* | [`move_money`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/move-money/) — both categories in one confirmation | W ↺ |

The forecast places YNAB's scheduled transactions on their dates, finds recurring
charges in your history, averages the rest, and lets you give your income and one-off
amounts. Guide: [Plan ahead](/avenir-mcp/guides/plan-ahead/).
How it works: [Forecasting](/avenir-mcp/concepts/forecasting/).

## Ready-made workflows

Four [prompts](/avenir-mcp/reference/resources-and-prompts/#prompts) walk the agent
through a whole task. In Claude Code they are slash commands of the `avenir-mcp` server.

| Prompt | Arguments | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `classify_pending` | plan | suggestions, your choices, one confirmed batch, the undo id |
| `monthly_review` | plan, month | totals, what stands out, proposed fixes |
| `reconcile` | plan, account, bank balance | the gap explained, then a confirmed reconciliation |
| `plan_next_month` | plan | forecast and assumptions, then next month's amounts |

## Built in

- **Safety:** read-only by default, confirmation bound to the exact preview, a local
  journal for undo, bank text treated as untrusted. See
  [Safety by design](/avenir-mcp/concepts/safety/).
- **Economy:** a local copy of your transactions refreshed with delta sync; most tools
  cost one or two YNAB requests. See [Data and sync](/avenir-mcp/concepts/data-and-sync/).
- **Clarity for agents:** currency units, typed output schemas with a description per
  field, pagination, actionable [errors](/avenir-mcp/reference/errors/).
- **Protocol:** MCP 2026-07-28 and earlier versions; stdio or streamable HTTP.
