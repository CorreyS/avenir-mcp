**Goal:** understand a month — income, spending, what is left to assign — and correct
what went off track.

**Tools:** [`get_monthly_summary`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/get-monthly-summary/),
[`get_category_balances`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/get-category-balances/),
[`get_budget_vs_actual`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/get-budget-vs-actual/),
[`get_spending_trends`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/get-spending-trends/),
[`get_savings_rate`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/get-savings-rate/),
[`get_age_of_money`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/get-age-of-money/), and
[`move_money`](/avenir-mcp/reference/tools/move-money/) to fix.
**Prompt:** `monthly_review`.

## The conversation

<Steps>

1. **Ask.**

   > **You:** Review September with me.

2. **The month at a glance.** `get_monthly_summary` with `month: "2026-09-01"` answers in
   a few lines — one YNAB request:

   <Code code={summary} lang="json" title="get_monthly_summary" />

   | Field | Reads as |
   |---|---|
   | `income` | money received this month into Ready to Assign |
   | `budgeted` | total assigned to categories this month |
   | `activity` | spent (negative) and received in categories |
   | `ready_to_assign` | money with no job yet — negative if you assigned more than you have |
   | `overspent` | categories whose available balance is negative |

3. **The detail.** `get_category_balances` lists each category with something in it:

   <Code code={balances} lang="json" title="get_category_balances (first entries)" />

   `get_budget_vs_actual` gives the share of each budget consumed — `utilization_pct`
   above 100 means overspent:

   <Code code={usage} lang="json" title="get_budget_vs_actual (first entries)" />

4. **The trend.** Is Restaurants high this month or every month?
   `get_spending_trends` with `months_count: 3` gives spending per category, month by
   month:

   <Code code={trends} lang="json" title="get_spending_trends (excerpt)" />

5. **The fix.** Overspending is covered by moving money from a category with room:

   > **Claude:** Restaurants is 22.50 over. Tennis has 80 available and nothing spent yet. Move
   > 30 from Tennis to Restaurants?
   >
   > **You:** Yes.

   One `move_money` call: both categories in one preview, one confirmation, one undo:

   <Code code={move} lang="json" title="move_money — preview" />

</Steps>

## How much did you keep?

Ask *"how much of my income do I keep?"*: `get_savings_rate` gives, for each of the last
complete months, the income (money put in Ready to Assign), the spending (money out of the
budget accounts, refunds deducted), what was saved, and the savings rate: saved over
income. Money moved between budget accounts, here the 200 put into Savings each month,
stays saved; a transfer to a tracking account that holds an asset (savings, investments)
is saved too, a loan payment is spending. Starting balances are not income.

Here the household kept 48.4 % of its salary from June to August, June its best month.
The notes say how each figure was counted: the agent relays them with the rate.

<Code code={savings} lang="json" title="get_savings_rate" />

## How old is your money?

Ask *"how old is my money?"*: `get_age_of_money` gives YNAB's Age of Money for each month
up to the current one, in days: how long, on average, money stayed in the budget accounts
before being spent, the oldest money spent first. Over 30 days, the household lives on
last month's income. The figure is YNAB's own, read with the list of months in a single
request: it is the one YNAB shows.

Here June has no figure: its payments came before the first salary, and YNAB needs some
history first. From 17 days in July, the money is 47 days old in September: what was
received in June and July still pays September's bills.

<Code code={age} lang="json" title="get_age_of_money" />

## Good to know

- `month` is `"current"` or the first day of a month, `YYYY-MM-01`. Anything else is
  refused before YNAB is called, at no cost:

  <Code code={badMonth} lang="text" title="get_monthly_summary — month 2026-13-01" />
- Hidden categories and YNAB's internal ones (*Inflow: Ready to Assign*) are left out of
  every review.
- `move_money` moves an **amount**: Tennis goes from 80 to 50 and Restaurants from 120 to 150
  together, and `undo_operation` puts both back. To set one category's total instead,
  `set_category_budget` takes an **absolute** amount.
- The `avenir-mcp://guide` resource reminds agents of the YNAB method: overspending is fixed
  by moving money, not ignored.
